# Acknowledgements

AnchorCache builds on and interoperates with several open-source projects. We thank their authors and contributors:

- [Qwen-Image](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-Image) for the Qwen image generation and editing models.
- [Hugging Face Diffusers](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers) for model, pipeline, scheduler, VAE, and PEFT integrations used by the image backends.
- [FireRed-Image-Edit](https://github.com/FireRedTeam/FireRed-Image-Edit) for the open-source image-editing training code that informed the original Qwen-Image-Edit data, model-loading, and training workflow.
- [FLUX](https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux) and Black Forest Labs for the FLUX model family.
- [OmniVoice](https://github.com/k2-fsa/OmniVoice) and the k2-fsa community for the multilingual TTS model, tokenizer, codec, and data pipeline.
- [FastVideo](https://github.com/hao-ai-lab/FastVideo) for its unified accelerated video generation inference and post-training framework.
- [MiniMax-H3](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3) for its open omni-modal generation system and multimodal generation workflows.
- [PyTorch](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch), [Transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers), [Accelerate](https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate), and [PEFT](https://github.com/huggingface/peft) for the underlying training and inference ecosystem.
